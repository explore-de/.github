# explore.de – Digital Thread Platform

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of [explore.de](https://www.explore.de/), the Digital Thread Platform that transforms complex data into actionable insights. Our mission is to make data accessible, interoperable, and valuable through the integration of Generative AI and Agentic Intelligence.

## 🚀 About explore

**explore** is a cloud-native platform designed to create intelligent Digital Twins by connecting and automating data flows across various systems. By leveraging our AI assistant, **Lora AI**, we enable organizations to:

- **Integrate** data from PLM, ERP, CRM, and other systems.
- **Automate** workflows and data transfers without manual intervention.
- **Interact** with data using natural language, making complex information easily accessible.
- **Visualize** data in real-time 3D representations for enhanced understanding.

Our platform ensures that data is not only collected but also contextualized and utilized effectively, fostering better decision-making and innovation.

## 🧠 Key Features

- **Lora AI Assistant**: Provides context-aware, natural language access to your data, facilitating tasks like data modeling, analysis, and troubleshooting.
- **Thread Composer**: Automates data flows between systems, supporting both event-based and periodic processes.
- **Open Standards**: Ensures full interoperability with existing systems, avoiding vendor lock-in.
- **Security & Compliance**: Implements robust security measures to protect your data and maintain compliance with industry standards.

## 📂 Repositories
